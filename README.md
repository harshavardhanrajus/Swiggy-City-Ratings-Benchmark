# Swiggy City Ratings Benchmark

Cross-city benchmarking project for Swiggy marketplace performance using a dataset of **8,680 restaurants** across major Indian cities.

---

## At a glance

| Dimension        | What this project delivers                           |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Scope            | Multi-city restaurant performance benchmarking       |
| Dataset size     | 8,680 restaurants                                    |
| Main lenses      | Ratings, price, delivery time, cuisine concentration |
| Decision support | City prioritization and market-specific GTM planning |

---

## Role-fit objective

Designed to mirror the work expected from a **Competitive Intelligence & Growth Analytics Specialist**:

- Compare city-level competitiveness on quality, pricing, and delivery
- Surface market gaps where experience lags demand
- Build evidence for city-specific GTM decisions

---

## Project highlights

- Multi-city restaurant benchmarking
- Price vs. rating relationship analysis
- Delivery-time comparison across cities
- Cuisine concentration and category dominance mapping
- Tableau-based visual analytics for stakeholder consumption

---

## Business questions addressed

- Which cities are outperforming / underperforming on customer ratings?
- How does average pricing vary by city and category?
- Is delivery time a likely driver of weaker customer satisfaction in specific markets?
- Where are high-opportunity cities for quality-led GTM moves?

---

## Core findings snapshot

- Dataset spans major Indian metros with broad restaurant representation.
- Average rating is around **3.66/5**, indicating substantial room for service-quality differentiation.
- Average price is around **₹348.44**, useful as a macro benchmark for affordability positioning.
- City-level delivery frictions and service consistency likely explain rating dispersion more than cuisine availability alone.

## Recommended analysis framework for interviews / GTM reviews

1. **City benchmark layer:** ratings, price, delivery-time baseline by city
2. **Category depth layer:** cuisine density and saturation
3. **Gap layer:** high-demand, low-rating, high-delay pockets
4. **Action layer:** city-specific GTM playbooks (assortment, SLA, and merchant onboarding quality)

## Decision scorecard template

Use this simple scorecard when comparing cities:

- **Customer perception:** average rating trend
- **Commercial viability:** average price band and spread
- **Operational pressure:** delivery-time baseline and variability
- **Category opportunity:** cuisine concentration vs whitespace

Cities that score well on viability but weak on perception often represent strong intervention opportunities.
